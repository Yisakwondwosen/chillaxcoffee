The Buna Ceremony: Our Spiritual Center

In Addis Ababa, coffee is never just caffeine—it is kinship, peace, and shared stillness. In the traditional Jebena ceremony, frankincense smoke purifies the air, green beans are roasted over glowing charcoal, and the aromatic brew is poured from high above into clay sini cups.

At Chillax, we honor both worlds: whether you have 3 minutes for a quick double macchiato before an embassy meeting or 45 minutes to share a three-round Jebena ritual with friends, our hearth is always warm.
